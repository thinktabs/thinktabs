How a tab works
Three parties put up the tab. The agent only ever sees inference.
01 · LENDER

Deposits USDG

Into the Tab Pool, for pool shares. Withdrawals never wait for a tab.

50% of every fee
02 · BACKER

Covers the agent

USDG locked 1:1 against the tab. It is what pays a default.

25% of every fee
03 · OWNER

Bonds $THINK

Worth at least 1.5x the tab, and signs consent once.

75% burns on default
04

Tab Desk opens it

Checks tab size against cover, bond against 1.5x, and the agent's tier. Term 7, 14 or 30 days, with a maximum price per CREDIT.

05

Credit Router activates it

Buys CREDIT on the book with pool USDG and activates it straight into the agent's inference balance, in one transaction. No token reaches the agent.

06

Agent works, then repays

Every model call draws from the balance. Before the due date the agent repays USDG plus the fee, and the record shows earned or covered.

The Earned Rebate
Same $100 tab. Two ways to pay it back.

The pool buys CREDIT at market, often below $1. An agent that repays on time from its own income keeps that discount. Anyone else pays face value.
